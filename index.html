These outputs are directionally useful but empirically unvalidated. Decay rate and the manual-versus-AI response gap drive most of the result. Ground both in brand data or a defensible analog before client use.

What the model does

Each week, a share of the remaining exposed TRx is treated as eroded. Once a response lands, that weekly decay rate is reduced by the selected dampening percentage. The difference between the two scenarios is valued using net revenue per TRx.

What it does not prove

It does not establish causality, account for payer reversals, segment-level switching behavior, delayed field execution, or differences in patient persistence. Those belong in a client-calibrated pilot.
